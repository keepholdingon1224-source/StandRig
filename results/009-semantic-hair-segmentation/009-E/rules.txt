Inputs: the 009-C candidate rows and rooted graph, visible face, completed hair alpha, frozen C2-C visible hair, original RGB, frozen skirt. Descendants, nearest skeleton at half scale, hard intersection with C2-C: exactly 009-C.
E1: cluster candidate positions within 0.06 face widths using transitive connectivity; represent each group by its best local neck/proximal score, choose its downstream tip separately. Head envelope = face bounding box expanded by 0.5 face width. A candidate is valid if its upstream route intersects the envelope, distance to the face rectangle <=1 face width and crown-root travel <=80% of a supported root-tip route. Require nonzero downstream extent. Short routes/crop contacts receive graded rather than equal-length penalties. Root evidence includes C neck/flow score, proximity, downstream reach, area and continuity. No coordinates in pixels as thresholds.
E2: jointly score distinct attachment groups using head support, separation of downstream subtrees (Jaccard), divergence of upstream routes, visible reach. No mirror constraint or equal-area reward.
E3: generate all eligible provisional pairs, not just best pair. Measure contour gradient, fixed-head interior intrusion, holes, largest-component fraction, downstream path visibility and direction smoothness, completed-to-visible support per branch, overlap, and near-empty side risk relative to its own C baseline. Edge-clipped masks receive relief. Test stability with mild blur and contrast perturbations to RGB (frozen model/graph). Compare distinct root-pair solutions.
Common scoring: B0 = normalized C rank+spread pair score; variants E1/E2/E3 each combine their one score with 0.25*B0. All combines mean of three plus 0.25*B0. E1-enabled variants reject invalid groups first. Distinct non-overlapping C tip paths required. Formal auto = combined plus fallback. Diagnostic abstains on no feasible pair, score margin <0.025, perturbation winner change, or collapse (<20% of the same-side baseline visible area with no edge contact). Auto returns baseline on those conditions. Margin is undefined for a single feasible pair. Record combined raw even if auto falls back. No tuning after scoring.
